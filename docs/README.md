# Reading the Register

The Rules, the Pledge, and the designated oracles bundle live inside the Register itself. They are not reproduced here.

To read them, join the Register:

**https://app.extol.work/groups/node-operator-register**

Non-signers welcome 🤟

## Independent verification

All three governing documents are stored on Arweave and anchored on Solana mainnet SAS. A reader who wants to verify independently can inspect the on-chain attestation record directly. The Register's coordinator channel will provide the Arweave transaction IDs and SAS attestation PDAs on request.
