# Reading the Register

The Rules, the Pledge, and the designated oracles bundle live inside the Register itself. They are not reproduced here.

To read them, join the Register:

**https://app.extol.work/groups/e0870a06-ef03-4373-84ac-6543be962ac5**

The membrane admits non-signers. Readers who want to understand what the Register asks of signatories, without pledging themselves, are welcome.

## Independent verification

All three governing documents are durably stored on Arweave and anchored on Solana mainnet SAS. A reader who wants to verify independently can inspect the on-chain attestation record directly. The Register's coordinator channel will provide the Arweave transaction IDs and SAS attestation PDAs on request.
